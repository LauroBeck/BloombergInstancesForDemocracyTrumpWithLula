## 📌 Project Overview

This repository aggregates structured SQL queries and analytical templates designed to monitor and model macro-financial data, institutional growth metrics, trade flows, and foreign direct investment (FDI) between Brazil and China.

### Core Focus Areas
* **Sino-Brazilian Trade Dynamics:** Strategic market expansion across agribusiness, energy transition, tech infrastructure, and manufacturing sectors.
* **Institutional Market Analysis:** Financial intelligence tracking key indicators across Latin American and Asian capital markets.
* **Media & Market Sentiment Integration:** Observational data frameworks tailored to media discourse across key financial and news outlets including **Bloomberg Intelligence, CNBC, CNN Brasil, BandNews, Globo, GloboNews, and g1**.
